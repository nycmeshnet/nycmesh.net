faq:
  - question: "Do you accept space, equipment, financial donations?"
    answer: "Yes! NYC Mesh accepts donations of working and reasonably modern networking equipment and antennas. We also welcome donations of space for meetups and events, and [monetary donations](/donate/) are tax-deductible."

  - question: "How do I learn more about NYC Mesh for a project of my own?"
    answer: "Join our [Slack](https://slack.nycmesh.net) and check out the [NYC Mesh Wiki](https://wiki.nycmesh.net) for documentation, guides, and technical resources."

  - question: "What days and times are new volunteer activities?"
    answer: "Our main weekly event is Open Mesh Night, every Tuesday at 7pm at 53 Bridge St, Unit 509, Brooklyn. We also hold monthly Meetups - check [Meetup.com/nycmesh](https://meetup.com/nycmesh) for dates."

  - question: "What type of volunteers are best for NYC Mesh?"
    answer: "All types! We welcome people with diverse skill sets - from technical networking and software to outreach, grant writing, and graphic design. Self-motivated people who enjoy collaborating do great here."

  - question: "Is there training for new volunteers?"
    answer: "Yes. New installers shadow experienced install leaders before leading installs themselves. We also offer onboarding calls and our Slack community is always happy to help newcomers learn."

  - question: "What do you currently need the most help with?"
    answer: "We always need mesh installers, network administrators, and outreach volunteers. Check our [Slack](https://slack.nycmesh.net) channels for the latest priorities."

  - question: "Do I need to be based in NYC?"
    answer: "For installation and maintenance work, yes. But for software development, design, outreach materials, grant writing, and other remote-friendly tasks, you can contribute from anywhere!"

  - question: "Who do I contact for more information?"
    answer: "Join our [Slack](https://slack.nycmesh.net) and post in #general or #new-volunteer-onboarding. You can also email us or come to an Open Mesh Night in person."

  - question: "Can I expand the network to my neighborhood?"
    answer: "Absolutely! Neighborhood organizers coordinate installs for their building, neighbors, and local businesses. Join #neighborhoods on Slack to get started."

  - question: "What is it like to volunteer with NYC Mesh?"
    answer: "It's a collaborative, welcoming community. Most volunteers don't have a specific 'job description' - we work on whatever projects we're interested in. You'll meet great people and learn a lot!"

  - question: "Do I need to have tech experience to volunteer?"
    answer: "Not at all. While tech skills are useful for some roles, we also need help with outreach, event coordination, grant writing, graphic design, and community organizing."

  - question: "Does NYC Mesh offer paid opportunities and internships?"
    answer: "NYC Mesh is primarily volunteer-run. Occasionally there are paid contractor positions for specific projects. Check Slack for any current openings."
